# Signature ability cards — v2

12 October 2026, updated after director feedback. Replace the plain text selector with four illustrated ability cards. Keep the exact combat effects, activation conditions, shared single slot and unlock requirements. A signature is a permanent, one-time choice for that fighter.

Give each ability a square Higgsfield illustration in the approved chunky cartoon 3D style, a readable name and effect, its activation condition, and the existing tradeoff description. Do not repeat “SIGNATURE ABILITY” beside every illustration. Mark abilities compatible with the saved career gameplan; this is information, never an automatic tactics change or a promise about a different Gym Wars team plan.

Locked fighters can inspect all four choices and their benefits. The two independent unlock requirements retain their completion states and direct training/fight links. Ready and live bouts block the first choice until fight night ends. Selecting a card previews a confirmation with its effect, activation condition and an explicit permanent-choice message. Cancel and Escape save nothing; a failed save leaves the choice available. After confirmation, show only the chosen ability as a permanent card. Remove clearing and switching controls. The engine rejects alternate IDs and null; an exact repeat preserves a single history entry. Existing valid signatures become permanent without changing their identity or historical fights. A published Gym Wars team still requires explicit republication.

Full fighter cards show the chosen ability's square art in the corner. Use one shared badge for roster, fighter detail, Home, Gym, training, scouting, fight posters, fight-night corners and Gym Wars team/arena/result presentations. The tooltip shows only the ability name and short effect. Support hover, focus, phone taps, Escape and outside dismissal. Render the tooltip outside card clipping and clamp it to the viewport. Keep badge buttons separate from card navigation controls. Recorded fight cards use the saved signature, never a later roster choice.
