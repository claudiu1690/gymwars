The fresh career completed its story and all three Gym Wars cups using only normal resources. The ledger reconciles. This is one scripted, randomly generated career, not evidence of human enjoyment or a causal balance comparison.

## Isolation and method

- New, initially empty database `gymwars_playthrough_v6_2026_10_14`, one newly registered synthetic account and a separate browser profile. The director's account/database was never connected.
- Registration through the public landing, normal $1,600/one Prospect/eight career weeks/one Gym Wars token. No fixtures, purchases, resource grants, prepared checkpoints, outcome retries or restored saves.
- Thirty-nine authorized return visits, each exactly +24 hours on this private server. Normal refill amounts/caps stayed four weeks/12 and one token/three. Real-time waiting was simulated; all career weeks used the visible manual End week control.
- Visible offers, scouting, camps, recovery, signatures, staff quests, mentoring and club challenges. After story completion, unfinished circuit honors were prioritized, then high guaranteed purses. This is a different policy and random roster from v5.
- At week 161, after repeated Summit losses and observed training ceilings, the policy changed the four published calls using visible matchup stats: ground approach, measured pace, composed finish. Existing outcomes remained recorded. No game source, seed, fighter stat or reward changed. This is an adaptive extension, not a matched-seed experiment proving tactics caused the eventual win.

## Designer assessment

**Working:** the story has a deliberate ending, normal progression remains solvent, earned milestones provide useful money, individual qualification prevents instant high-circuit entry, and published tactical choices matter enough to be worth exploring. A trained Prospect can contribute to the final cup; no rarity purchase or stat boost was required.

**Still worth improving before the game freeze:**

1. Make training ceilings unmistakable before assigning another camp. Several fighters plateaued and repeated camps could not improve their cup prospects. Lead with the expected gain and offer another focus, preparation/recovery or scouting without removing any strategic option.
2. Put the next sporting goal closer to Home's primary action after the story. The illustrated goals work, but the current layout places them below club goal/reputation/team coverage panels on desktop. The active fight stays the first action; the next cup/proving night should be its clear secondary goal.
3. Review the purpose of late repeat contracts. The post-story segment took 94 weeks and 97 bouts, with 46 wins/51 losses. The policy intentionally preferred guarantees after unfinished honors and retained a weak veteran rather than recruiting upgrades; that is not an optimized profit strategy or proof every player must grind this much. Still, profitable repeated losses and zero-growth camps are poor default habits to encourage. Show the expected sporting/development payoff as clearly as the purse.

There were 63 quiet End week results and 99 important reports. Keep the manual shared calendar; improve its meaningful next action rather than introducing another timer or removing choices. Compared with v5, story completion was week 68 vs 66; HQ was 126 vs 144 and coach rank 5 was 128 vs 144. Different rosters, random outcomes, goal policy and the late tactics extension prevent attributing these differences solely to the new prizes or title rules.
