# Gym Wars project preferences

## Video generation

- Use **720p** for future generated videos unless the user explicitly requests a higher resolution.
- For vertical reels, generate and export at **720 × 1280 (9:16)**. For landscape videos, use **1280 × 720**.
- Select 720p in the generation provider's request; generating at 1080p and then downscaling does not save generation credits.
- Keep titles and overlays readable at 720p. Do not upscale final videos merely to label them 1080p.
- This preference applies to future work; existing video deliverables do not need to be regenerated.

## Marketing branding

- Brand marketing materials as Gym Wars. Omit company/studio credit lines unless the user explicitly requests them.
