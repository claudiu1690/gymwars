## Tested behavior

- Real registration, setup, sign-out and login preserve a normal fresh career.
- Training assignments save without advancing time. Custom allocation validation, camp review, manual weeks, permanent gains, preparation and recovery work.
- Investigation and recruitment assemble four weight divisions and unlock ranked Gym Wars.
- Booking, individual rounds, reload and coach finish persist one result, purse and ranking change.
- Regional title wins/defenses, the three-fighter Invitational, construction/deed gates, specialist rooms, earned staff promotion, mentoring and the final World Open reward work from their documented checkpoints.
- Optional goals explain their payoff and can be changed or unpinned without removing fighter progress or supplies. Elite signatures remain permanent.
- Fighter release requires a division-loss warning; missing coverage locks ranked play. Retirement preserves the fighter record.
- Four fighter tactics and reserve selections persist and become the actual accepted battle inputs. Two separate authenticated test gyms attack each other in real 4v4 cards.
- Leaderboard totals agree with accepted attack receipts. Defense history, rival following, token exhaustion and saved result replay work. Eleven real ranked cards demonstrate best-ten scoring; season rollover resets season points while retaining history and lifetime points.
- Achievement cosmetics equip for free and display in Gym Wars. Pack checkout grants fixed supplies once, shared interval limits apply, and a lost response recovers its existing receipt.
- Trusted refill time, stale revisions, exact command replay, private exports/reports, password rotation and revoked sessions behave as expected.
- Seventeen destinations render at desktop and mobile sizes without page overflow, dropdowns, accordions or checkmark glyphs. Compact navigation dismisses with Escape.
- Production-mode checkout remains disconnected and cannot grant supplies. External email is unavailable in the isolated environment.

No application defect was reproduced in the final tested paths, so this work changes test infrastructure rather than game mechanics. During suite development, failures were corrected selectors, fixture assumptions, account-switch setup and a deterministic combat checkpoint; those are not claimed as game fixes.

The test runner always creates disposable databases and synthetic accounts. The actual playtest account was not reset or written to. Reports are local generated artifacts and the next browser run replaces them. CI now includes the suite and retains reports, but hosted CI has not been executed as part of this local run.

## Limits

There are 30 distinct scenarios, with 18 repeated at the mobile viewport. Late progression uses annotated seeded checkpoints, including stronger fighters and supplied prior milestones; it is not an organic start-to-finish affordability or balance test. The season loop also uses real API commands and a private server clock rather than waiting real days.

Human comprehension, fun and retention, full-career economy pacing, Firefox/WebKit, real devices, Internet latency/concurrency/load, external email and live payments still need separate validation. Stripe remains disconnected.
