# Organic playthrough improvements — implementation v2

14 October 2026. Implements the recommendations in [the original proposal](organic-playthrough-improvements-v1.md), based on [the continuous unboosted playthrough](../testing/organic-playthrough-2026-10-14.md).

## Design intent

Make the repeated prepare / End week / fight loop easier to read and faster to operate. Keep allocations, specialist rooms, duration, recovery, tactics, four weight divisions, permanent signatures and sporting progression. Manual End week remains the only calendar action. No dropdowns, extra currencies, cosmetic sales or paid progression gates were added.

## Balance pilot and limits

**Follow-through:** [Combat and career incentives — balance v3](combat-economy-balance-v3.md) implements the subsequent approved finish/defense and earnings changes. The figures below remain the original v2 pilot record; current controlled comparisons and validation are in the linked follow-through.

`scripts/studyOrganicBalance.cjs` runs three policies across three shared fresh-career seeds, for 28 normal daily visits each: 8 opening weeks, then 4/day with unchanged caps, no purchases/grants/save injection. This is an engine lab, separate from the earlier browser playthrough and from the user’s account.

All nine runs reached week 116 with zero hardship weeks. Sporting play completed the career at weeks 60, 76 and 63. Highest-guarantee play completed it in one of three runs (week 93); development play completed none within the observation window. Median end cash was $17,040 / $39,590 / $22,495 respectively. Median net saved cash per four game weeks was $532 / $1,310 / $721, including capital spending. Policies follow different objectives; these are not human completion forecasts or an optimal-strategy proof. Guarantee chasing remains lucrative. The shared-demand rule addresses division rotation, not every possible incentive; further global purse cuts are not justified by insolvency evidence.

A separate synthetic study runs 17,500 seeded bouts: seven skill gaps, five approach/condition/trait profiles, 500 paired seeds per profile. In the high-pace boxing comparison, a 60-skill fighter won 47.6% against equal skills, 27.0% against all-80 skills and 25.4% against all-90 skills. Large upsets are therefore a repeatable balance concern rather than one odd organic result. The alternate grappling/measured profile is not claimed to be an optimal counter. Do not infer signature balance or all style matchups from these profiles. Preserve existing replays; propose and playtest any future finish-probability adjustment as a separately versioned combat change.

Human first-session comprehension, enjoyment, reasons to return, a 5–8-person usability study and genuine retention remain unvalidated. Automated normal returns do not prove retention. Stripe remains disconnected.
