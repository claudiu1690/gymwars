# Design Review 23 — implementation contract

Review date: 13 October 2026. Source: GymWars / Design Reviews / Design Review 23 in Notion. Keep the original player notes intact.

1. Locker next fight: one opponent panel with portrait, event, date and a labeled coach read. Remove the duplicated assignment paragraph when a bout is booked. Preserve preparation and tactical entry points.
2. Signature guidance: a padded, styled footer, including the existing permanence and Gym Wars publication reminders.
3. Traits: group revealed tendencies under “Fighter traits”, with their actual combat effects. Never disclose hidden traits.
4. Skills: four visual permanent skill tiles, numeric ratings and meters. Keep temporary preparation separate and preserve observed plateau information.
5. Roster coverage: use fighter portraits instead of checkmarks. Remove its redundant Challenge a gym action.
6. Roster save footer: show Career saved instead of leaving Week complete behind. Preserve the explicit End week button and its team results report.
7. Career Path: distinguish six club reputation unlock levels from four regional fighter title ladders. Explain and link the separate 4v4 Gym Wars leaderboard. Existing opponents, purse progression and title eligibility stay intact.
8. Staff: replace aggregate camp/investigation quotas with role-specific sporting quests. Show quests, gym requirement and cash together by Promote; use one through five stars for ranks. See staff-promotion-quests.md.
9. Gym Wars: selected opponent uses the blue pressed state without an added checkmark or plus.
10. Goals: rename player-facing ambitions to optional Club Goals. Manage them on Career Path, track them on Home and relevant fighter lockers, and retain the fight-result update. Explain automatic progress, normal rewards and changing goals. Future challenges show their prerequisite and link back to the unlock path.

Acceptance: all ten notes implemented, no dropdowns or information accordions, no decorative button arrows, desktop and phone layouts verified, automated mechanics and presentation tests passing, current player save preserved, next review created under the existing Notion hub.
