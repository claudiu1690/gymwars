# Staff promotion quests — v2

Money funds a promotion after the staff member has earned it through play. Keep the existing rank benefits, fees, upkeep and gym requirements.

| Next rank | Coach | Scout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Finish qualifying camps with two different fighters; win a career bout with a coached fighter after camp | Investigate three weight classes; a signed recruit completes a career debut |
| 3 | Finish camps covering three primary skill focuses (balanced is a focus); three coached fighters win career bouts | Investigate all four divisions; two signed recruits win career bouts |
| 4 | Win the Career Invitational with at least two prepared fighters; coached fighters earn belts in two divisions | Investigate all three fighting style families; a signed recruit earns a regional belt |
| 5 | Coached fighters earn belts in three divisions; two different coached champions defend | Signed recruits earn belts in two divisions; a signed recruit defends a belt |

A qualifying camp lasts at least three weeks and finishes in full while employed; the current offered full camps are four or six weeks. Assignment, cancellation, early taper, recovery-only weeks and repeated one-week camps do not satisfy it. Distinct fighters/divisions/focuses prevent farming a single action. Wins count only after the recorded preparation, and Invitationals require preparation before their event. Vacant belt allocation is not a title bout.

The engine owns proof recording, never client-supplied counters. Investigation proofs record prospect, division and style family; recruitment proofs record fighter identity; completed camp proofs record fighter, date and focus. Career bout receipts and Invitational results establish sporting outcomes. Work is cumulative while the staff member is employed; releasing staff resets that staff member's dossier.

Old saves keep existing ranks and aggregate counters. Dated last completed camps and signed histories after hiring can establish genuine existing proof; ambiguous aggregate counters alone cannot establish these new quests. Projection is read-only. No account reset or free promotion is required.

Promotion UI shows both quests and all financial/facility gates beside its action. Rank stars increase from one to five. The engine enforces exactly the same gates and spends once without advancing time.
