# Game interaction rules

Updated 12 October 2026 from the director's explicit feedback: **no dropdowns or information accordions anywhere in the game.**

- Show decision choices as visible, labeled cards or buttons. Do not introduce native selects, custom dropdown menus, details/summary disclosures, or inline expand/collapse information panels.
- Use a small number of explicit tabs for distinct screens. Gym Wars uses Battle, Team, Club and Leaderboard. Switching screens preserves unsaved team selection and tactics.
- Put actionable matchup information directly beside the battle flow. Show results and key exchanges directly, without an extra reveal button for information.
- Use a focused dialog for an optional editing task such as changing an ambition, setting custom tactics or reviewing week plans. Include a heading and Close action, support Escape, contain keyboard focus, and restore focus to the originating button.
- Show permanent information such as staff progression and camp repeat previews as visible sections and cards.
- Keep strategic choices, rules and rewards intact. Reduce interaction steps and hidden information.
- Use plain action labels without decorative arrows. Retain arrows only where they communicate a numerical change, a calendar span or another factual relationship.
- The responsive navigation menu is navigation, not an information accordion; retain it on narrow screens.
