# GymWars Mechanics Feeling and Return Hook v2

**Date:** 9 October 2026  
**Status:** Design recommendations. The proposed daily allowances and strict four-division team are not live rules.

## Core experience

The strongest loop is recruit a fighter with a recognizable identity, choose an opponent, prepare for a specific weakness, fight and see a meaningful consequence. Preserve the four skills, styles, fatigue/recovery tradeoff, rarities and manual rounds/automatic option.

The team layer should turn individual progress into a club ambition: four fighters, one in each division, competing against another complete corner. Extra fighters are reserves and successors. Do not make separate attack/defense squads or rarity sets mandatory.

## Daily pacing and economy

The initial proposal supplies eight career weeks, then four every 24 hours, with a twelve-week bank. Ranked cards start at one, add one daily and bank three. These values need economy and session tests before enforcement. Calendar spending must bound all permanent training, recurring recruiting and construction opportunities; ranked cards replenish independently of End week.

One isolated opening scenario reached its first standard bout at week four and a second at week eight, with a win followed by a loss and positive cash. That supports trying the opening budget, but does not establish that eight clicks feel good or that all seeds complete the intended opening.

Reliable missing-division scouting and Starter capacity four must accompany the lock. Current Starter capacity is three. In an isolated capacity-four recruiting probe, 88.4% of 1,000 accounts covered all four classes by week two; the slowest took seven. Random coverage is feasible but can consume a valuable daily allowance without an enjoyable decision. Guarantee useful missing divisions rather than relying on that tail.

### Later cash flow

Maximum HQ plus coach/scout upkeep is $385 per game week. The table assumes exactly four fighters, each fighting once every four weeks; no delays, recruitment or purchase spending. Actual mixed results, six-week offers and training can change throughput.

| Illustrative four-fighter outcome | Team gross per game week | Net after $385 upkeep |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary fighters all lose; $180 show each | $180 | −$205 |
| Ordinary fighters all win; $400–$530 each | $400–$530 | $15–$145 |
| Four title wins; $1,400 each | $1,400 | $1,015 |

This exposes a large ordinary-versus-champion earning step. It is not a measured average or a promised time to max the gym. All expansions, rooms and staff currently cost $140,300 before upkeep/recruitment. The 36 construction weeks alone need at least seven daily refill intervals under the proposed initial supply; earning cash can take much longer.

Recommended: extend earned career purse progression through competition tiers, or test a modest sponsorship baseline earned by club achievement. Current purses principally distinguish ordinary and title bouts; richer circuits would be new content. Prefer extending that fighting loop if it can cover the gap; avoid inventing another currency or a collection of passive jobs. Run four-fighter 30/50/70% win policies at each tier, checking net cash, hardship frequency, manual fight workload and time to a useful purchase. Players should not need fourteen earning fighters to sustain a simple daily experience.

## Training and mentoring

Fractional growth, public plateau discoveries and actual upgrade previews are now correct. Preserve them. Long camps use the same weekly growth/fatigue and ordinary completion bonus as short ones, so their main benefits are commitment and fewer reassignments. Under a daily budget, a six-week commitment crosses visits without clearly better efficiency.

First test whether scheduling and halfway decisions make that choice worthwhile. If not, prototype one modest completion benefit for four/six weeks, such as +1/+2 above the existing next-bout bonus, non-stacking with replacement/expiry rules. Those are candidate test values. Do not increase both permanent growth efficiency and bonus at once. An alternative is to honestly present longer camps as scheduling convenience; do not claim a strategic benefit the rules do not provide.

Mentoring's four/six-week lessons and guaranteed +2 preparation are improvements. Both participants are reserved, but other fighters still progress. Show the commitment and budget clearly. Starting only requires one funded week, so warn when current cash and already-booked earnings do not support the lesson; keep intentional risk available.

The available-lesson generator currently includes all known traits, including suspect chin and big-fight nerves. Normal mentoring should transfer useful or clearly explained situational traits. Exclude pure drawbacks from new lessons on both UI and server. Do not transform old results or remove character flaws from existing fighters.

## Combat

Keep the broad engine. Existing controlled balance diagnostics support style and stat relevance; current feedback exposes actual rounds, gas costs and preparation. Three quick calls plus editable advanced instructions are an appropriate complexity level.

At zero gas, the new finish commitment can pay zero while retaining the full hunting multiplier. Test this edge case alongside tired-leading, hurt-opponent and final-round situations. If the benefit needs to scale with available effort, introduce a versioned change. This does not establish that Hunt dominates all combat, nor justify another complete redesign.

Make outcomes emotionally readable: short decisive exchanges, a visible verdict and the actual progress earned. Preserve the full log for inspection and instant finish for fast play. A loss should point toward one factual improvement or recovery action, without pretending one stat difference alone caused a probabilistic outcome.

## Gym Wars fairness and purpose

Current scored play accepts one to four fighters, resolves only overlapping divisions and can grant three points for one winning bout. End week creates another scored opportunity. Implement the agreed complete four-division card and real-time gate before competitive release.

Keep fresh-team rules and immutable defenses. Career recovery should not force reserve squads. Show what is published and what has changed. Release/retirement of a last division representative needs a clear warning/replacement path; historic cards remain unchanged.

The lobby currently offers three authored clubs and the latest 24 defenses, with no comparable-power/rank selection or repeat-opponent restriction. A daily card cap does not stop choosing the same easy club each day. Assess strongest eligible owned division coverage, standing and repeat history; do not let a weak published defense manufacture easier matching. Show a small understandable rival selection before commitment. Exact thresholds need combat and population testing.

Current standings add attacker points across all time. Separate permanent 6/15/30 club honors from a bounded competitive standings period. Options are a simple season leaderboard (lower scope) or recurring small division groups (more rivals/context, larger scope and population needs). Start with one competition structure after matching works; do not add both. Preserve previous history and label pre-gate scoring as preseason.

Defense results need a return payoff on Home, even while they do not grant attack points. Keep that scoring separation to avoid unlimited passive rewards from repeated attacks. Show a factual new result, available card and nearest attainable club goal. No fake messages or online statuses.

## Content and long-term growth

The campaign currently ends after one fixed three-bout Invitational. Rename it Career Invitational, preserve its existing rules for now and stop describing the separate league as only exhibitions. A later account needs a specific next challenge rather than generic continuation.

Give selected major gym chapters a new earned opponent/cup opportunity, a successor challenge or a visible club identity reward. Existing six-tier goals and badges are a useful start. Attach rewards to distinct experiences rather than adding ranks and percentages. Aging is too distant to supply the first weeks of return motivation, so do not depend on natural retirement for early content.

Legacy is currently a prominent accumulated score. Either make its club-reputation milestones and purpose explicit or reduce its HUD prominence until it has a meaningful role. Keep cash as the spendable resource.

## Release criteria

- Every fresh account can assemble four legal divisions within the initial allowance without expansion or a rare roll.
- First waits occur after a meaningful result; free early sparring and planning remain usable at zero supply.
- Complete league cards always resolve four bouts; retries, tabs and lineup edits cannot create supply or points.
- Ordinary four-fighter policies have viable operation and visible investment progress at intended tiers.
- A returning player identifies one changed club outcome and one useful action within ten seconds.
- A post-Invitational account has a concrete challenge and reward.
- Players can explain when to choose a long camp, rest, a reserve or composed tactics; no added screens are needed to compensate for unclear value.
