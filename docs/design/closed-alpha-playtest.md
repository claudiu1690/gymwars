# Closed alpha playtest — v1

## Purpose

Validate understanding and the attachment/return hook before expanding content. Automated scripts establish functional behavior; they cannot answer whether a new player understands the game or wants another session. Recruit 5–8 new players through the director. No invitations or messages have been sent by this implementation.

## First visit (uncoached)

Give each participant a fresh, isolated staging account and ask: “Build your corner and complete your first fight.” Do not point to controls or explain game rules. Let participants stop voluntarily. Record screen/actions only with their agreement; use participant codes, not emails, in the shared findings.

Record elapsed time and assistance at these milestones: booking an opponent/date, choosing training, understanding that assignment saves a plan, manually ending a week, playing rounds and reading the result. Also record the earliest wrong turn, expected outcome of the click, recovery path and any control they cannot locate. Ask them to think aloud without coaching the choice. Training focus, duration, rest and tactics stay theirs.

Targets, not achieved results: at least 80% complete those actions without assistance; median time to first result below eight minutes. With a small sample, report raw counts as well as percentages. Do not use speed as a reason to remove strategic choices. Fix the earliest repeated confusion, then retest with new participants.

After the result ask: “What helped or hurt here? What would you change next?” Count a success only if they identify at least one recorded preparation, readiness or tactical fact. Do not require a particular strategy or imply the outcome was guaranteed. Include a close win, loss and 2–2 Gym War across the sessions. The two result-feedback buttons and Help report queue capture explicit feedback; they are not a substitute for observation.

## Return visits

Invite consenting participants to return at their own convenience across 7–14 days. Record first second fighter/full corner/expansion/belt/staff promotion, chosen goals, bank arrival balance, reason for stopping and intended next action. Include a player who waits several days and a player who spends all supply. Observe whether the fourth division expands choices or causes administration. Ask whether the next camp, ranked card, rival or sporting quest gives them a concrete reason to return.

Do not reward a forced login streak. Offline time must never play career weeks or charge upkeep. Track repeat opponents and whether matched power bands produce useful choices. Test reserve swaps, changed/stale defenses and late-season entry. The league's accepted results and rules stay immutable.

## Evidence sheet

For each participant: code; build/date; device/input; consent; prior genre familiarity; milestone times; assistance count; first confusion; preparation/result explanation; chosen next goal; stopping reason; return observations; report reference. Keep raw quotes and observations separate from designer interpretation. Do not populate this sheet with simulated players.

Aggregate only the opted-in funnel and eligible return cohorts using the operations report tool. Those cohorts start on first observed opt-in visit, so they are not a complete acquisition cohort or a causal retention experiment. Review blocked reasons alongside explicit reports. Actual D1/D7/D30, comprehension, fun, live population and demand for additional authored rivals remain pending human evidence.
